This is a fully static website: it does not call any AI and does not collect your data. Where AI is needed (reviewing answers and explaining), the site generates a prompt, and you copy it into your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another one).

## Studying a lesson [#lesson]

1. Before you start, look at the prerequisites listed under "Review before the lesson". Click "Copy review prompt" and the AI writes a few short warm-up questions on the concepts you need to practice (from your learner profile; if there are none yet, on the prerequisites of the lesson). The review is not scored.
2. Read the explanations in order. In the interactive figures you can move the sliders and see how the conclusions change with the parameters.
3. When you do not understand a passage, select the text with the mouse and click the "Explain this" button that appears. The site generates an explanation prompt that contains the selected text, its paragraph and the key points of the lesson.
4. The course has three kinds of questions:
   - **Check questions** follow the sections of a lesson and check that you understood each section. The AI reviews them without scores.
   - **Exercises** come at the end of each lesson. The AI reviews them without scores.
   - **Quizzes** come after each group of lessons. They are scored.

## Answering and submitting [#grading]

1. Write your answers to the check questions on paper (or type them).
2. Below the lesson summary, under "Submit the check questions", click "Copy feedback prompt".
3. Paste the prompt into your AI assistant, then attach a photo of your answers or type your answers after the prompt, and send it.

You can submit all your answers at once or in several parts. The AI reviews only the questions you submitted this time and marks each one "OK" or "needs revision". Questions you have not submitted are shown as "not answered", and questions that were already reviewed keep their earlier result. Each question shows its result next to it.

Exercises are submitted the same way, with the button below the exercises.

## Saving results [#results]

The feedback prompt asks the AI to end its reply with a result block:

```text
<<<LFWAI-RESULT
{"v":1,"lesson":"0.3","items":[{"id":"C3","status":"ok"}],"mastered":["covariance"],"weak":[],"note":"..."}
LFWAI-RESULT>>>
```

Paste this block (or the whole reply) into the "Paste the result from your AI" box and click save. The result of each question ("OK" or "needs revision"), the concepts to practice and the note are added to your learner profile.

## Learner profile [#profile]

Your learner profile is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is never uploaded.

- **Feedback prompts do not include your profile.** The review looks only at your answers.
- **Explanation prompts** include your background, your learning preferences and the concepts from this lesson that you need to practice, so that the AI can adapt its explanation. They do not include scores. You can turn this off on the <a href="/learn-finance-with-ai/en/guide/profile/">learner profile</a> page.
- On the learner profile page you can also copy a "session start prompt". Send it first when you start a new study conversation, so that you do not need to repeat it later in the same conversation.
- Export your profile before you change device or browser. You can also upload the exported file to a project in your AI assistant (a Claude Project, a Gemini Gem, and so on) as long-term context.

## About correctness [#accuracy]

All lessons, questions and reference answers on this site were generated by AI and have not been reviewed by a professional. Their correctness is not guaranteed. Every feedback prompt tells the AI that the reference answer itself may be wrong, and that it should point out the error and review the answer against the correct one. If you find an error, please use the "Report an error on this page" link at the bottom of the page.

The content is for learning only and is not investment advice. Content about tax rates, contribution limits, interest rates and other rules or data changes over time; always check the current official information.
