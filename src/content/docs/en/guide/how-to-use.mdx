This is a fully static website: it does not call any AI and does not collect your data. Where AI is needed (grading and explanations), the site generates a prompt, and you copy it into your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another one).

## Studying a lesson [#lesson]

1. Read the explanations in order. In the interactive figures you can move the sliders and see how the conclusions change with the parameters.
2. When you do not understand a passage, select the text with the mouse and click the "Explain this" button that appears. The site generates an explanation prompt that contains the selected text, its paragraph and the key points of the lesson.
3. Check questions follow each section, and each lesson ends with exercises. After a group of lessons there is a quiz.

## Answering and submitting [#grading]

1. Write your answers to the review and check questions on paper (or type them).
2. Below the lesson summary, under "Submit the review and check questions", click "Copy grading prompt".
3. Paste the prompt into your AI assistant, then attach a photo of your answers or type your answers after the prompt, and send it.

You can submit some questions now and the rest later. Questions you have not submitted do not get 0 points; they are shown as "not done yet". When you submit more later, questions that were already graded keep their scores.

Exercises are submitted the same way, with the "Copy review prompt" button below the exercises. Exercises are reviewed by the AI and are not scored: each one is marked "OK" or "needs revision".

After each group of lessons there is a quiz, which is scored.

## Saving results [#results]

The grading prompt asks the AI to end its reply with a result block:

```text
<<<LFWAI-RESULT
{"v":1,"lesson":"0.3","items":[{"id":"C3","score":2,"max":3}],"mastered":["covariance"],"weak":[],"note":"..."}
LFWAI-RESULT>>>
```

Paste this block (or the whole reply) into the "Paste the result from your AI" box and click save. The scores (for exercises, "OK" or "needs revision"), the concepts to practice and the note are added to your learner profile.

## Learner profile [#profile]

Your learner profile is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is never uploaded.

- **Grading prompts do not include your profile.** Grading looks only at your answers.
- **Explanation prompts** include your background, your learning preferences and the concepts from this lesson that you need to practice, so that the AI can adapt its explanation. They do not include scores. You can turn this off on the <a href="/learn-finance-with-ai/en/guide/profile/">learner profile</a> page.
- On the learner profile page you can also copy a "session start prompt". Send it first when you start a new study conversation, so that you do not need to repeat it later in the same conversation.
- Export your profile before you change device or browser. You can also upload the exported file to a project in your AI assistant (a Claude Project, a Gemini Gem, and so on) as long-term context.

## About correctness [#accuracy]

All lessons, questions and reference answers on this site were generated by AI and have not been reviewed by a professional. Their correctness is not guaranteed. Every grading prompt tells the grading AI that the reference answer itself may be wrong, and that it should point out the error and grade against the correct answer. If you find an error, please use the "Report an error on this page" link at the bottom of the page.

The content is for learning only and is not investment advice. Content about tax rates, contribution limits, interest rates and other rules or data changes over time; always check the current official information.
