The course aims at the level of an upper-undergraduate economics student (intermediate microeconomics, intermediate macroeconomics, investments and corporate finance). Its finance content roughly covers CFA Level I, and the final goal is to build portfolio models for US households. Lessons are published over time; unpublished lessons show only their titles.
