## After the course you will be able to [#skills]

- Analyze firms and markets like an economist: how competition, incentives and asymmetric information shape prices and profits.
- Explain how interest rates, inflation, the business cycle and monetary policy move the prices of different assets.
- Read financial statements and value bonds and stocks.
- Explain why diversification works, why risk and expected return are linked, and why most actively managed funds trail their index.
- Build and test a portfolio model for a US household, including accounts and taxes, asset allocation and retirement withdrawals.

The depth is about that of upper-level undergraduate courses in economics and finance.
